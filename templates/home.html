Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for more than one opportunity?

Yes. Your dashboard keeps your applications and their current statuses organized.

Can employers manage applicants?

Yes. Employers can review candidate applications and update statuses such as reviewing, shortlisted, interview, selected or rejected.

Is the recommendation system AI-powered?

No. Recommendations are based on transparent profile and job attributes such as skills, category, location and experience level.
